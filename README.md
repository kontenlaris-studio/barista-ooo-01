The last out-of-office note from an AI that quit the inbox to become a barista: your email is now a latte it will not be making.

## Why this line

The AI is the narrator, and it has genuinely left: the email is no longer an unread message to triage, it is a drink order at a cafe where nobody is coming back to make it. The joke lands on the swap — inbox duty becomes cafe duty, and the email becomes the latte.

